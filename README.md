# Zomato
The dataset contains restaurant details from Zomato, including location, cuisines, ratings, votes, and service features like online delivery and table booking. It helps analyze restaurant perfomance, customer preferences, and food trends across different cities

# Pros
Wide Restaurant Variety - Offers multiple cuisines and dining options in one place.

User Reviews & Ratings - Helps customers make informed decisions

Online Delivery & Booking - Convenient food ordering and table reservations.

Location-Based search - Easily find nearby restaurants.

Exclusive Offers & Discounts - Saves money through deals and promotions.

# Cons
Missing Values - Some fields like ratings or cost may be incomplete

Inconsistent Data - Variations in cuisine names or formats.

Outdated Information - Restaurant details may not reflect current status

Biased Ratings - Ratings can be subjective and not fully reliable.

Limited Features - May not include deeper insights like customer demographics or order history

# Project Insight
The analysis highlights key trends in restaurant performance using data from Zomato. It shows that a few top restaurants receive a significantly higher number of votes, indicating strong customer preference and brand popularity.

Cuisine-based insights reveal that certain cuisines dominate in both availability and customer ratings, helping identify popular food trends. City-wise distribution indicates that restaurant concentration is higher in major cities, reflecting urban demand.

The dashboard also shows that a large number of restaurants offer online delivery, while fewer provide table booking services. Additionally, average ratings across cuisines help evaluate customer satisfaction levels.

Overall, the project provides valuable insights into customer behavior, restaurant performance, and market trends, supporting better decision-making in the food industry.

# Conclusion
The analysis of restaurant data from Zomato provides clear insights into customer preferences, popular cuisines, and restaurant performance across cities. It highlights the importance of ratings, votes, and service features like online delivery in influencing customer choices.

Overall, the dashboard helps understand market trends, identify top-performing restaurants, and supports data-driven decision-making in the food industry.
